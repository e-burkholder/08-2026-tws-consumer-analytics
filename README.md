## Objectives
This project aims to analyze how consumers interact with TWS across streaming platforms, social media, goods/merchandise, and other touchpoints. This project focuses on analyzing data from their most recent album *No Tragedy*. After analyzing the results from their latest comeback, the project aims to determine how a group like TWS could break into the Western market and whether it would produce worthwhile commercial returns.

I aim to identify:
1. Did *No Tragedy* perform well as a comeback album?
2. Who are the core audiences of TWS?
2. How do consumers discover and engage with TWS?
3. What consumer behaviors correlate with high engagement or purchases?
4. Given TWS's branding, which US consumer segment offers the greatest opportunity?
5. Where does the consumer segment stand within the industry? 
6. How could this information be utilized to improve marketing efforts in the US market?
7. How could future comeback marketing campaigns better target this audience?
8. Would targeting US consumers return commerical value worth the costs?

The group debuted as a 6 member group on January 22, 2024 with the mini album *Sparkling Blue*. [(Pledis Entertainment, Profile)](https://www.pledis.co.kr/ko/artist/detail/TWS/profile/)
The members are:

- Dohoon (도훈)
- Youngjae (영재)
- Hanjin (한진)
- Jihoon (지훈)
- Kyungmin (경민)

This helps us establish who the group is and the basic information utilized for their branding.
